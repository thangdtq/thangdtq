## About Me

IT Support professional focused on end-user computing, hardware troubleshooting, and IT infrastructure.

My primary interests include maintaining workstation environments, diagnosing hardware and peripheral issues, supporting office IT operations, and troubleshooting network connectivity.

I am particularly interested in the infrastructure side of IT — understanding how endpoints, network devices, and system services work together to maintain a reliable working environment.

Currently expanding my knowledge beyond Helpdesk operations toward **System Administration and Infrastructure Management**, with a long-term goal of growing into a System Administrator role.
